# Reports

This directory contains compact, reviewed reports and aggregate tables. Raw
experiment logs, private-data outputs, OOF arrays, and unreviewed generated
figures do not belong here. The synthetic benchmark is generated by public
code; competition data and historical prediction artifacts are not included.

See the [results and evidence status](../docs/results.md),
[method and variant catalog](method_catalog.csv), and
[curated-results index](curated_results/README.md). `curated_results/`
contains selected historical aggregate comparisons described in
[`docs/results.md`](../docs/results.md).
[`experiment_index.csv`](experiment_index.csv) is a title-and-provenance index
for 131 research reports retained in the private working archive.

The `MTH-*` values are catalog keys assigned for this release, not original
experiment IDs. A row represents a report, which may describe a variant,
feasibility screen, audit, or repeated branch; the rows are not 131 unique
methods or independent trials. `source_sha256` fingerprints the corresponding
local report. The source notes and their machine-specific locations are not
published, so a digest alone cannot reproduce or independently verify a result.

Every inventory row is marked `HISTORICAL_INDEX_ONLY`. The CSV intentionally
does not infer causality, validation validity, effect size, matched control,
or final disposition by scanning report text. The public status of selected
results appears in [`docs/results.md`](../docs/results.md), and family-level
interpretation appears in [`docs/method_catalog.md`](../docs/method_catalog.md)
and [`docs/failed_experiments.md`](../docs/failed_experiments.md). No private
competition data, predictions, model files, or logs are included.
