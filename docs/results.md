# Results and evidence status

This page keeps three different kinds of evidence separate. The synthetic benchmark is reproducible from the public repository. The official competition score is a verified aggregate from a private evaluation, but the underlying data are unavailable. Historical research scores describe exploratory work and are not clean benchmark estimates.

## Clean, reproducible synthetic benchmark

The fixed benchmark compares three streaming detectors on six generated break mechanisms and an unchanged control. Each process has a 256-observation reference history and a 512-observation stream; the break occurs at stream index 256. The generator, detector implementations, complete metrics, and seeds are in [`breakforge.validation.synthetic`](../src/breakforge/validation/synthetic.py) and [`synthetic_benchmark.csv`](../reports/synthetic_benchmark.csv).

| Generated mechanism | Raw level CUSUM AUC | Conditional innovation CUSUM AUC | BreakForge reference AUC | BreakForge detection rate | BreakForge median delay |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mean shift | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | 0.950 | 22.0 |
| Variance shift | 0.8406 | 0.9663 | 1.0000 | 0.950 | 33.5 |
| AR coefficient shift | 0.2844 | 0.2938 | 0.9006 | 0.675 | 150.0 |
| Persistence reversal | 0.6431 | 0.4806 | 1.0000 | 0.950 | 15.0 |
| Frequency shift | 0.0175 | 0.0238 | 0.8288 | 0.550 | 168.0 |
| Heavy-tail shift | 0.4244 | 0.4838 | 0.4781 | 0.075 | 17.0 |
| No change | — | — | — | 0.025 false-positive rate | — |

On the unchanged evaluation streams, the raw level CUSUM false-positive rate was 0.125 and both conditional detectors had a rate of 0.025. Thresholds were set separately for each detector at the nearest-rank 95th percentile of 80 independent generated null streams. The evaluation used another 40 null streams and 40 streams per changed mechanism. AUC is the pairwise ranking probability of final stream scores against those null controls, counting ties as one half. Detection rate counts a trace only when it crosses the calibrated threshold after the known break and had not already crossed before it. Delay is the median number of observations after the break among detected traces.

Reproduce the checked-in table with:

```bash
python scripts/synthetic_benchmark.py \
  --seed 20261005 \
  --repetitions 40 \
  --calibration-repetitions 80 \
  --history-length 256 \
  --stream-length 512 \
  --output reports/synthetic_benchmark.csv
```

The table was generated by code revision `9d0244d` with the public reference configuration (`allowance: 0.25`); its data provenance is entirely synthetic. The sample is intentionally small: false-positive rates move in increments of 0.025, no uncertainty intervals are estimated, and each mechanism is one chosen data-generating process. The results demonstrate implementation behavior, not broad performance or transfer.

## Official competition result

| Competition | Metric | Verified private-evaluation score | Rank |
|---|---|---:|---|
| ADIA Lab / CrunchDAO Structural Break: Real-Time (2026) | Time-stratified AUC | **0.6213427008** | Not independently verified |

The aggregate score is supported by a preserved provider receipt and run readback. The recorded run used the exact-stream competition package path. That historical submission was a different, more elaborate system from the compact Gaussian AR(1) detector currently exposed as BreakForge's public reference API; the score does not measure the current public detector. No run identifier, submission bundle, predictions, series identifiers, or competition data are published. This one private evaluation is not a reproducible public benchmark, does not establish a final rank, and does not show that the method generalizes beyond the competition distribution. Competition data remain subject to the platform's no-redistribution rule; see [competition background and data policy](competition.md).

The CV-A rows are historical, competition-derived aggregate findings. Their original data, fold IDs, predictions, and model files are not included. The available records do not provide a complete public manifest of code revision, seeds, configuration, and fit IDs for every row, so these results cannot be independently reproduced from this repository. The score and caveats are preserved to make the research record honest, not to claim generalization.

### One-time holdout diagnostics

An internal grouped-ID CV-B diagnostic was inspected once. A contemporaneous access log records that no model selection was performed after that score read, but it also documents later metadata-only exposure of the holdout manifest and artifact paths. Because the split is no longer an untouched, sealed final holdout and the complete provenance is not part of this public repository, its values are not included in the clean benchmark table and must not be used for further tuning or as a sealed-holdout estimate.
