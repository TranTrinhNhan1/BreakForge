# Failed, inconclusive, and deprioritized experiments

This retrospective records configurations that did not justify promotion to the compact reference implementation. It does not disprove any broad method family. The selected aggregate values and their caveats are in [results.md](results.md); the wider map of approaches is in the [method catalog](method_catalog.md).

## Matched controls

Complex methods should be compared with a control that keeps the information set and engineering choices fixed. For a windowed representation, keep the same window, scaling, and sample count, then replace the proposed feature with low-order moments or autocorrelation. For a conditional score, compare raw and normalized streams with the same sequential detector. For learned features, hold outer folds, training IDs, feature budget, and decision head fixed. For a sequential test, use the same per-step input and calibrate each detector on independent null data.

One direct matched control compared a Trial 11 reference with a small D3 sequential-RFF addition on the same saved folds. The observed CV-A mean difference was only `+0.0001320` and the reduced diagnostic difference was `+0.0005989`; these values are `VALID_EXACT_STREAM; NON_NESTED_META_CV; POST_SELECTION_CV`. The DMD/Koopman blend had a higher selected CV-A mean (`0.6356422` versus `0.6344382`) but a lower reduced diagnostic (`0.5918742` versus `0.5996029`); it carries the same labels. These comparisons helped deprioritize the added complexity; they are not clean estimates of the methods' effects.

This project found comparisons where a richer representation added little over a simpler matched control. Those comparisons were affected by repeated model selection, so they remain qualitative research lessons rather than clean performance claims. Full raw scores and private identifiers are not needed to understand the lesson and are not redistributed.

## Reading negative evidence

- A low or inconsistent delta means that tested configuration did not earn promotion under its available protocol.
- A partial-fold result cannot be generalized to the omitted folds.
- A high CV score does not override a lower transfer diagnostic, nested-fold contamination, or repeated selection.
- `VALID_EXACT_STREAM` applies to inference causality only. It does not repair training-time contamination.
- No family in this table is declared universally ineffective.

See [validation.md](validation.md) for leakage categories and [the curated archive index](../research_archive/README.md) for the research questions retained in this release.

## Late-stage matched controls and diagnostic corrections

The final September audit added a matched-capacity P2+Aux ablation. The 47-input no-age head and 48-input age head scored above the Clean Baseline V2 reference on exposed CV-A folds, but the no-age recipe's one reduced-only diagnostic fell below its matched control. The component-level prefix audit passed, while full integrated inference parity remained open. The result is labeled `CLOUD_PRIVATE; POST_SELECTION_CV; FEATURE_COMPONENT_PARITY_ONLY; PACKAGE_PARITY_UNKNOWN`; the reduced diagnostic also carries `REDUCED_ONLY`. Detailed values and resampling limits are in [results](results.md#late-p2aux-attribution-and-transfer-diagnostic).

A static history-ACF feature passed a held-seed synthetic mean-effect check but failed a preregistered condition for the persistent-history group, so that fixed recipe was closed. This is `SYNTHETIC_STAGE0_HELD_SEED`, not part of the public reproducible benchmark. A separate online-age and historical-group analysis found descriptive ranking differences but did not establish a cause or justify tuning from those exposed subgroups (`CLOUD_PRIVATE; DESCRIPTIVE_OOF_DIAGNOSTIC`). ACF-decile accounting likewise did not support cross-stream score calibration for a detector that receives one series at a time (`CLOUD_PRIVATE; EXPLORATORY_METRIC_AUDIT`).

Two additional history-only rank maps were tested on synthetic Rosenblatt streams. Ranking each fixed-window component before the mixture had a positive heavy-tail contrast but missed its frozen advancement threshold; ranking the integrated score afterward was below the raw score on all four held seeds. Both implementations passed the recorded prefix-invariance and deterministic-regeneration checks. The calibration windows overlap, so exchangeability with future windows was not established; neither result supports a valid p-value, e-value, or sequential guarantee. Both fixed recipes were closed as `SYNTHETIC_ONLY; FROZEN_GATE_FAILED` and were not added to the public benchmark (`SRC-035; SRC-036`).

The robust-history-null proposal also failed its matched-control gate: the tested correction did not improve specificity. A later aggregate OOF check did not support the claim that jump-contaminated histories were under-detected. These are configuration-specific findings (`SYNTHETIC_STAGE0; MATCHED_CONTROL` and `CLOUD_PRIVATE; OOF_DIAGNOSTIC`), not a rejection of robust calibration as a family.

Finally, a synthetic break-magnitude screen initially suggested a low-signal ceiling, but its raw maximum score increased with stream age under null. The follow-up therefore downgraded the threshold-based ceiling claim; only its threshold-free ranking results remain suggestive for the tested generator and detector. This is `SYNTHETIC_STAGE0; PRIVATE_BREAK_MAGNITUDE_DIAGNOSTIC; INTERPRETATION_CORRECTED`, not a general DGP limit.

## Late method-stage screens

A Clean V2 direct head augmented with OSN-RFF and full-scan U features scored 0.6060691 versus 0.5992524 for its matched five-fold CV-A control (reported mean delta +0.0068168; `CLOUD_PRIVATE; POST_SELECTION_CV; VALID_EXACT_STREAM`). Its one-shot reduced diagnostic was 0.5323959 versus 0.5205256 (`CLOUD_PRIVATE; POST_SELECTION_CV; REDUCED_ONLY`). Feature/cache parity was exact, but prior CV-A exposure and the exposed reduced split prevent an independent transfer claim; the candidate was not promoted. Full fold vectors and limits are in [results](results.md#additional-source-report-outcomes) and the method entry [`SUP-012`](method_catalog.md).

The AR candidate-location mixture did not improve its max-GLR control: the uniform-duration mean was 0.5317707 versus 0.5329225 and the duration-weighted mean was 0.5244552 (`CLOUD_PRIVATE; POST_SELECTION_CV; VALID_EXACT_STREAM`). A separate exact all-start AR screen had near-chance standalone evidence and negative fixed-blend deltas on its two transfer folds (`CLOUD_PRIVATE; PARTIAL_FOLD; POST_SELECTION_CV; VALID_EXACT_STREAM`). A richer nine-expert Student-t/EWMA bank lost to the Gaussian AR bank on all four source synthetic seed rotations (equal-seed means 0.6542221 versus 0.6576308; `SYNTHETIC_ONLY`). These are configuration-level outcomes; the full comparisons are recorded in [results](results.md#additional-source-report-outcomes) and [`SUP-013`–`SUP-015`](method_catalog.md).

The P3 stage-two optimizer selected a candidate using a two-fold objective, then its full five-fold replay scored 0.629066 versus 0.631890 for the trial-13 reference (`CLOUD_PRIVATE; POST_SELECTION_CV`). The partial search objective did not survive the broader replay, and no stage-two candidate was promoted ([`SUP-016`](method_catalog.md)). Separate convolution, parallelism, and threading audits are indexed as runtime studies; their speed measurements are not detector-performance results.
